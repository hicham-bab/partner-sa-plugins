# Certifications and the partner portal

Show **both** certifications in every partner-facing session, as a standard act 5 block — not only when
the stated outcome is certification. Upskilling is the reason partners invest in a practice, and the
partner portal is where the funding and content already sit. Leaving it out means the partner leaves
motivated with no path.

## The two certifications

| | dbt Analytics Engineering Certification | dbt Architect Certification |
|---|---|---|
| Audience | Analytics engineers | Enterprise architects, tech leads |
| Focus | Analytics engineering workflows, modelling, testing, deployment, documentation | Platform security, permissions, monitoring, governance, dbt Mesh, dbt Catalog |
| Prerequisite | dbt Fundamentals badge | dbt Analytics Engineering Certification |
| Also referred to as | dbt Certified Developer | dbt Certified Architect |

Present them as a **ladder, not a menu**: Fundamentals badge → Analytics Engineering → Architect. The
prerequisite chain is the story, and it makes the investment legible to a partner planning staffing.

## Exam language — verify per audience

Availability differs by exam and language, and older study guides saying "English only" are out of date. As of
early 2026: the Analytics Engineering exam is localised in **Japanese**; the Architect exam is available in
**French**, while the AE exam was not yet. No evidence of Spanish, Italian, or German localisation.

Verify before any non-English session claims anything here, and state the exam language explicitly on the
slide. See `languages.md`.

## Logistics — verify before presenting

Recorded from internal enablement material. Prices, versions, and formats drift, so confirm current values
in the partner portal or with the training team before a slide states them.

- Format: online proctored, registered through Talview — `https://pages.talview.com/dbtlabs/certifications/`
- Analytics Engineering exam: 2 hours, 65 questions, 65% pass mark
- List price: $200 per attempt; SI partner discount available (commonly 20%)
- Certification validity: 2 years from award date
- Exam vouchers: $200 each, expire one year from purchase
- Discount codes: one certification each, redeem within 90 days of issuance
- The Analytics Engineering exam tracks a specific dbt Core version — **check the current version in the
  study guide** rather than stating one from memory
- Support: `certification@dbtlabs.com`, `#dbt-certification` in Slack

available to them there:

- Request exam vouchers centrally, so staff don't pay out of pocket

  `https://partners.getdbt.com/px/-/credentials-dashboard`

## The one thing that makes this slide work

Internal certification-programme data shows the drop-off is **not** in training completion — it's at exam
registration. Cohorts complete the learning and then never book the exam.

So the certification block should never be a bare list of exams. Always include:

1. The ladder, with prerequisites made explicit
2. Where the funding and content live — the partner portal
3. **An agreed exam-by date**, or a prompt to set one in the session

Point 3 is what converts. A certification slide without a date is a slide about intentions. Where the
session format allows, make setting exam-by dates a live activity rather than a follow-up.

## Slide placement

| Session type | Placement |
|---|---|
| Enablement | Act 5, before next steps — standard, every session |
| Hackathon / workshop | Act 5, framed as "where to go from what you just built" |
| Outcome = pass certification | Expand to 2–3 slides: ladder, pathway timeline, exam-by commitment |

For architect-track audiences, note that the Architect certification content maps closely to the platform
administration topics the session already covered — that continuity is itself an argument for sitting it.
